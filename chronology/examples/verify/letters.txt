Harbour Mill Letters (transcribed)

Letter 1. 14 March 1887.
Dear Thomas, the new loom arrived on the 9th of March and the men
have been at it day and night. Father says we shall have the second
shed roofed before Whitsun.Letter 2. 2 August 1887.
The strike began on Monday, 25 July 1887, when the carders walked
out over the new piece-rates. Mr. Hale refused to meet them until
the Thursday following.Letter 3. 30 November 1888.
The mill burned on the night of 12 November 1888. Nobody was hurt,
thank God, but the north shed and every bale in it are gone. Father
means to rebuild in the spring.
