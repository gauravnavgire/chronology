# Apollo 11 - study notes

1. On 25 May 1961, President Kennedy told a joint session of Congress that the
   nation should commit to landing a man on the Moon before the decade was out.

2. Apollo 11 lifted off from Launch Complex 39A at 13:32 UTC on 16 July 1969,
   carrying Neil Armstrong, Buzz Aldrin and Michael Collins.

3. Collins stayed in lunar orbit aboard the command module Columbia.

4. The lunar module Eagle touched down in the Sea of Tranquility on 20 July
   1969 at 20:17:40 UTC. Armstrong radioed: "The Eagle has landed."

5. Armstrong stepped onto the surface at 02:56:15 UTC on 21 July: "One small
   step for [a] man..."

6. Eagle's ascent stage lifted off at 17:54 UTC on 21 July to rejoin Columbia.

7. Columbia splashed down in the Pacific at 16:50:35 UTC on 24 July and was
   recovered by USS Hornet. The crew then spent 17 days in quarantine after
   splashdown.

8. Around 600 million people watched the July broadcasts worldwide.

9. The crew left a plaque reading "We came in peace for all mankind."
